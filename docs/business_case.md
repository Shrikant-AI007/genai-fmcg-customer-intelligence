# Business Case

## Objective
Create a business-facing AI copilot that reduces the effort required to investigate customer, product, sales, promotion and inventory questions.

## Primary users
- Sales managers
- Category managers
- Revenue management teams
- Supply-chain planners
- Business analysts

## Example KPI layer
Revenue, units, orders, average order value, customer monetary value, repeat purchase frequency, product contribution and inventory days of cover.

## Production roadmap
1. Connect ERP/CRM/POS data.
2. Add governed semantic layer.
3. Add vector database and enterprise document ingestion.
4. Add evaluation dataset and human feedback loop.
5. Add role-based access and audit logs.
6. Add monitoring for retrieval quality, hallucination, latency and cost.
